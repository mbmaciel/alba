# Alba System - Product Overview

## Project Purpose
Alba is a comprehensive business management system built in Python with a modern GUI interface. It provides complete functionality for managing business operations including customer relationships, inventory, financial transactions, and administrative tasks.

## Key Features and Capabilities

### Core Business Management
- **Customer & Contact Management**: Complete customer database with contact information, departments, and communication details
- **Company & Entity Management**: Multi-company support with detailed company profiles and configurations
- **User Administration**: User management system with role-based access control
- **Address & Location Management**: Geographic data management with CEP (postal code) integration

### Financial & Fiscal Operations
- **Tax Management**: NCM codes, CFOP operations, tax nature definitions
- **Invoice Processing**: Complete invoice generation and item management
- **Purchase Orders**: Order creation, tracking, and item management
- **Budget & Quotations**: Quotation system with detailed pricing
- **Billing Orders**: Comprehensive billing and faturamento management

### Product & Inventory
- **Product Catalog**: Fiscal product management with detailed categorization
- **Product Groups**: Hierarchical product organization
- **Production Items**: Manufacturing and production tracking
- **Commission Management**: Sales commission calculation and tracking

### System Administration
- **Configuration Management**: System-wide configuration settings
- **Text Templates**: Customizable text templates for documents
- **Activity Types**: Business activity classification
- **Customer Types**: Customer categorization system

## Target Users and Use Cases

### Primary Users
- **Business Administrators**: Complete system management and configuration
- **Sales Teams**: Customer management, quotations, and order processing
- **Financial Staff**: Invoice processing, tax management, and billing
- **Operations Teams**: Inventory management and production tracking

### Key Use Cases
- Small to medium business management
- Multi-location business operations
- Brazilian fiscal compliance (NCM, CFOP, tax regulations)
- Customer relationship management
- Financial transaction processing
- Inventory and production management

## Value Proposition
Alba provides an integrated business management solution specifically designed for Brazilian market requirements, offering fiscal compliance, multi-company support, and comprehensive business process automation in a modern, user-friendly interface.
